THE PRODUCT

Management mechanics wrapped in personality.

The game combines quick decision-making with a playful haunted-hotel identity. Guests arrive with clues, the player chooses rooms, mistakes cost lives and progression opens into collectible and challenge systems.

Core loop

  • Read each ghost guest's clues.
  • Choose the room that matches their preference.
  • Build score, streak and hotel standing.
  • Recover from poor shifts through the graveyard challenge.
