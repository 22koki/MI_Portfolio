THE CHALLENGE

From friction to a usable product.

Traditional market shopping can mean crowds, travel time, uncertain stock and fragmented communication. Vendors also need a simple way to receive orders without abandoning channels they already use.

The solution

TUKO Market is designed around local fulfilment: customers shop nearby inventory, vendors confirm and prepare orders, riders handle delivery, and the platform keeps payment and fulfilment states connected.

  • Designed around real operational flows
  • Structured to grow beyond a simple demo
  • Built to communicate business value, not only technical depth
PRODUCT DEPTH

What the system does.

01

Customer experience

Nearby discovery, basket building, pickup or delivery and order tracking.

02

Vendor workspace

Store, product, stock and order management with fulfilment controls.

03

Rider workflow

Delivery jobs, pickup verification, delivery confirmation and earnings tracking.

04

M-PESA

Payment architecture designed around Kenyan payment habits and settlement preferences.

05

WhatsApp

Messaging is treated as a first-class interface to the same backend workflows.

06

Smart basket direction

Future optimization can balance availability, distance, price, ratings and delivery cost.
