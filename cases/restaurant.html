THE CHALLENGE

From friction to a usable product.

Restaurants commonly split ordering, inventory, purchasing and reporting across disconnected tools. That makes it harder to understand what is happening operationally and where losses occur.

The solution

The platform is being shaped as a unified operating layer: menu items and orders connect to ingredients and inventory, while purchasing and finance-related records support the management side.

  • Designed around real operational flows
  • Structured to grow beyond a simple demo
  • Built to communicate business value, not only technical depth
