THE CHALLENGE

From friction to a usable product.

A farmer, gardener or landscaper may notice spots, yellowing, wilting or pest damage without knowing the cause. Generic search results can be overwhelming and image recognition alone can be overconfident.

The solution

Plant Doctor AR turns an image into a structured diagnostic experience: identify visible symptoms, suggest likely causes with confidence and severity, explain why, then provide treatment and prevention guidance supported by trusted research.

  • Designed around real operational flows
  • Structured to grow beyond a simple demo
  • Built to communicate business value, not only technical depth
PRODUCT DEPTH

What the system does.

01

Flexible scanning

Plants, crops, flowers, lawns/grass and trees/shrubs can follow adapted scan flows.

02

AI diagnosis

Likely condition, symptom analysis, confidence, severity and alternatives.

03

Explainability

The product explains which visual characteristics contributed to the suggested diagnosis.

04

Research layer

Treatment and prevention guidance is designed to be traceable to authoritative sources.

05

History

Structured plant-health records can be stored for later review and reporting.

06

Professional positioning

Designed as an assistive diagnostic tool—not a gamified or overconfident replacement for experts.
