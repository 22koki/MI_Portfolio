THE CHALLENGE

From friction to a usable product.

Spa operations often become fragmented across calls, notebooks, spreadsheets, payment messages and separate calendars. That creates scheduling conflicts, poor visibility and unnecessary manual work.

The solution

I designed the system around the actual flow of a spa: clients browse services, book therapists, staff manage appointments, payments are recorded, receipts are generated, and access changes depending on role.

  • Designed around real operational flows
  • Structured to grow beyond a simple demo
  • Built to communicate business value, not only technical depth
PRODUCT DEPTH

What the system does.

01

Booking engine

Client booking with therapist assignment, future-date validation and appointment overlap safeguards.

02

Payments

M-PESA STK, Till, cash and card-oriented payment workflows with confirmation and receipts.

03

Role-aware data

Clients, therapists and administrators see different operational records according to their role.

04

Service operations

Structured services, therapists, appointments and payment history in a single system.

05

Security

JWT authentication and server-side ownership/role enforcement rather than trusting the browser.

06

Commercial roadmap

Designed to expand into availability, cancellations, rescheduling, reporting and multi-location operations.
