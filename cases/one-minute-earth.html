THE IDEA

Make travel discovery feel like a product, not a directory.

Instead of presenting destinations as static cards, the experience uses surprise, timing, passport stamps and deeper destination pages to create a stronger sense of exploration.

Product direction

  • Discover destinations through timed or surprise flows.
  • Save favorites and history locally.
  • Use destination detail to teach culture, food, language and place facts.
  • Make the interface feel playful without losing clarity.
PRODUCT DEPTH

More than a travel gallery.

01

Surprise discovery

Randomized and themed exploration paths help users find places they would not have searched for directly.

02

Digital passport

Visited destinations become a visual record of exploration and give the experience a sense of progression.

03

Destination learning

Language greetings, food, cultural details, music, flags and local facts turn browsing into lightweight learning.
